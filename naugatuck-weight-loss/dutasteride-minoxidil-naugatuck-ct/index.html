    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Naugatuck, CT

    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Naugatuck

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Because it hits both, it lowers DHT in the blood by more than 90 percent, compared with roughly 70 percent for finasteride, which blocks only type 2. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil works on growth, not hormones. The pair covers cause and growth.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride. A year of steady finasteride with photos is the usual bar before stepping up. Fast early loss can move the timeline up. Women of childbearing potential cannot use it.

    Dosing and Schedule in Naugatuck

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Because it has a half-life of about five weeks, some providers prescribe it daily while others use a lower or less frequent schedule, such as a few times a week, to balance effect and side effects. Minoxidil goes on topically once or twice a day. Low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label alternative.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. The early shed is normal and usually fades. Around month six, density often improves. In head-to-head studies, dutasteride 0.5 mg produced greater increases in hair count than finasteride 1 mg over six months, which is why it is the common step up. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Dutasteride + Minoxidil in Naugatuck

    Dutasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in a small share of men, similar to finasteride. The long half-life means side effects can take weeks to months to clear. Mood changes should be reported right away. Minoxidil can irritate the scalp. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, mood history, sexual health and current medications. PSA numbers drop by roughly half, which your other doctors should factor in. Blood donation is paused for six months after stopping. Pregnant partners should not touch leaking capsules.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Naugatuck

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the usual first step, and both parts have FDA approval for hair loss. The dutasteride pairing is more potent, off-label and longer-lasting in the body. Men avoiding DHT blockers can use a minoxidil and GHK-Cu blend. Surgery is an option for some.
